\section{Introduction}

This document describes the design and implementation of \textbf{SmartGrid}, a distributed
smart-power-grid management platform built as a set of independently deployable \textbf{Spring
Boot} microservices coordinated exclusively through \textbf{Apache Kafka} (single-broker
\textbf{KRaft} mode, no ZooKeeper), complemented by a \textbf{Spark Structured Streaming} job
that provides real-time district-level analytics and an optional \textbf{C++/MPI} simulator able
to inject synthetic telemetry at scale.

The guiding design principle is \textbf{event sourcing via choreography}: every service owns
exactly one Kafka topic as its append-only write-ahead log, exposes a REST API backed purely by
an in-memory projection of that log, and rebuilds this projection from offset zero on startup
before serving a single request. No service ever calls another service synchronously over REST to
read or validate its data; cross-service knowledge --- e.g. \texttt{node-manager} confirming that
a \texttt{userId} exists before accepting a new node --- is obtained exclusively by subscribing to
the owning service's topic and maintaining a private, read-only materialized view of it. This
keeps every service independently deployable, restart-safe, and free of synchronous
inter-service coupling, at the cost of eventual (rather than immediate) cross-service
consistency.

\subsection*{Design Goals}
Table~\ref{tab:goals} summarizes the platform's four main design goals and the mechanism that
realizes each of them.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{0.16\linewidth} X X@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Goal} & \textbf{Guarantee} & \textbf{Mechanism} \\
\midrule
Durability &
  Any service can be killed and restarted with zero data loss &
  \texttt{@EventListener(ApplicationReadyEvent)} replays the service's own topic from offset 0
  into memory before the REST layer accepts traffic \\
Decoupling &
  Services never block on one another; a slow or down peer cannot cascade &
  Cross-service reads are served by a private, live-updated cache built from a consumed topic
  (e.g. Node Manager's cache of \texttt{user-events}), never a synchronous REST call \\
Real-time analytics &
  Every district's average energy balance and battery charge level stay continuously up to date &
  The analytics service, a Spark Structured Streaming job, continuously reads
  \texttt{measurement-events} and keeps two figures updated: a sliding-window average and a
  capacity-bounded running total \\
Load \& scale testing &
  Synthetic, high-volume telemetry, plus a like-for-like comparison of task-allocation strategies &
  A standalone C++/MPI simulator publishes directly to \texttt{measurement-events} under three
  selectable strategies, one of which (\texttt{node\_partition}) uses a real \texttt{MPI\_Allreduce}
  to combine cross-rank contributions \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\caption{Design goals and their implementation.}
\label{tab:goals}
\end{table}
